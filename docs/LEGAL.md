# What content is legitimate to add

HomeStream is a media *server* — the same category as owning a shelf of
Blu-rays and a good TV. What you put on it is what matters.

## ✅ Fine to host on your own server

- **Your own rips** of DVDs/Blu-rays you bought (personal-use format-shifting;
  rules vary by country — check yours).
- **DRM-free digital purchases** you own.
- **Public-domain films.** Huge legal libraries exist:
  - The Internet Archive Feature Films collection: <https://archive.org/details/feature_films>
  - Public Domain Movies: <https://publicdomainmovie.net>
- **Creative-Commons / open content**, e.g. the Blender open movies
  (Big Buck Bunny, Sintel, Tears of Steel) — great for testing your setup.
- **Home videos and personal media.**

## ❌ Not something this project does

The Reddit/GitHub "ultimate Plex stack" guides bolt on Radarr/Sonarr/Prowlarr
wired to torrent/usenet clients to auto-download current movies and TV. That is
copyright infringement in most jurisdictions, so this repo deliberately does not
include or configure it. HomeStream stops at the server + UI + hosting.

If you want a large legal library, the honest routes are: rip discs you own,
buy DRM-free, or subscribe to a service — there's no free-and-legal shortcut to
"any movie ever, instantly."
